South of Agartala lies the historical heart of Tripura: the paired destinations of Melaghar and Udaipur. Steeped in centuries of royal history and spiritual devotion, this picturesque region showcases two of the state's most celebrated treasures—the floating marvel of Neermahal on Rudrasagar Lake and the sacred temple sanctums of Udaipur, the former royal capital once known as Rangamati.

Surrounded by rolling green paddies, dense rubber plantations, and tranquil waterways, a journey through Melaghar and Udaipur reveals the poetic soul of Tripura. From cruising across shimmering waters to step foot onto the arched balconies of a majestic summer palace, to hearing temple bells reverberate across ancient sacred lakes, this trip pairs architectural majesty with profound cultural immersion.

## How to Reach Melaghar and Udaipur

By Air: The nearest airport is Maharaja Bir Bikram Airport (IXA) in Agartala, located approximately 55 km from Melaghar and 50 km from Udaipur. Taxis and rental cars can be hired directly from the airport to cover both towns within 1.5 to 2 hours.
By Train: Udaipur Railway Station (UDPU) is situated right on the Agartala-Sabroom railway line, offering frequent local and express passenger trains connecting to Agartala, Dharmanagar, and Silchar.
By Road: Both towns are well-connected by smooth, paved highways. Melaghar is around 53 km south of Agartala via the Sonamura route, while Udaipur is roughly 50 km along National Highway 8 (NH-8). Melaghar and Udaipur are just 25 km apart from each other, making them an effortless same-day or two-day road trip circuit.

## 2-Day Itinerary for Melaghar and Udaipur

### Day 1: The Floating Majesty of Neermahal (Melaghar)

Depart early from Agartala or Udaipur and drive down through scenic countryside roads to reach **Melaghar** and the shores of **Rudrasagar Lake** (a designated Ramsar wetland site).
Head to Rajghat on the lake's bank and board a traditional motorized boat or speed craft for a delightful 15-minute cruise across the water towards **Neermahal**.
As you approach, marvel at the palace's shimmering white and crimson sandstone walls rising dramatically out of the water. Constructed between 1930 and 1938 as the royal summer retreat of Maharaja Bir Bikram Kishore Manikya, the palace features an ingenious blend of ornate Hindu craftsmanship and graceful Islamic Mughal domes and arches.
Explore the palace's eastern and western wings: walk through the *Andar Mahal* (inner royal quarters), climb the open-air terraces for panoramic 360-degree views of migratory water birds circling Rudrasagar Lake, and observe the water pavilion steps designed for royal boats to anchor inside the palace halls.
In the late afternoon, enjoy tea and snacks at the lakeside tourist lodge while watching the golden sunset bathe the palace walls, before taking a short 35-minute drive to Udaipur for your overnight stay.

### Day 2: The Temple City and Sacred Lakes (Udaipur)

Begin your morning with an early darshan at the holy **Tripura Sundari Temple (Matabari)**. Considered one of the holiest 51 Shakti Peethas where Goddess Sati's right foot is believed to have fallen, this 16th-century red-brick temple features an idol of Goddess Kali carved out of black chlorite stone.
Step behind the temple sanctum to the banks of **Kalyan Sagar**, a sprawling historical lake teeming with large freshwater fish and rare, endangered Bostami softshell turtles (*Nilssonia nigricans*). Visitors traditionally feed puffed rice (*muri*) to the sacred turtles from the stone steps.
Next, visit the historic **Bhubaneswari Temple**, situated on the northern bank of the Gomati River. Built in the late 17th century by Maharaja Govinda Manikya, this temple provided the dramatic backdrop for Rabindranath Tagore's celebrated literary works *Bisharjan* and *Rajarshi*.
Explore the nearby **Gunabati Group of Temples**, dating back to 1668 AD, displaying unique Bengali stupa and terracotta architectural elements.
Before leaving, take a peaceful nature stroll through **Tepania Eco Park**, located just 5 km outside Udaipur, featuring wooden treehouses, orchidariums, and tranquil forest trails.

## FAQs and Tips for Visiting Melaghar and Udaipur

The ideal season to explore Melaghar and Udaipur is from October to March when pleasant winter breezes make lake boat rides and temple explorations delightful, and Rudrasagar Lake welcomes migratory waterfowl.
Boat tickets for Neermahal can be booked directly at the official Tripura Tourism ticket counter located at Rajghat, Melaghar. Shared boats and private speedboats are both available.
Dress modestly when visiting the Tripura Sundari Temple and remove footwear before entering the temple compound. Matabari is particularly vibrant and crowded during Diwali, when an expansive annual fair attracts lakhs of devotees.
Indulge in Udaipur's famous milk sweets, particularly the authentic local *Peda* sold fresh in the bazaar stalls lining the entrance of Tripura Sundari Temple.
Private auto-rickshaws and cabs are readily available at Udaipur railway station and central motor stands to shuttle between Melaghar and Udaipur's heritage spots.
